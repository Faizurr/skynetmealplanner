**[Restaurant legal name]** ("we") uses a face-recognition attendance system to record when you start and finish work.

**What we collect.** Your name, staff ID, job details, the time and place you check in and out, and, if you agree, a **face
template**: a set of 128 numbers calculated from your face. A template is not a photograph. If you also agree, we keep one
small reference photo so a manager can confirm your identity.

**Why.** Only to record attendance and work hours, calculate attendance reports, and prevent someone else checking in for
you. We do not use your face data for any other purpose, we do not use it to train AI models, and we do not share it with
other companies, except our service providers who host the system for us under contract.

**Your choice.** Using your face is voluntary. If you do not agree, or change your mind later, you can use a **staff PIN**
or ask a manager to record attendance. This will not affect your employment.

**How long.** Face data is deleted when you ask, when you are re-registered, or when your employment ends. Attendance
records are kept for **[X years]** as required for employment and payroll records.

**Security.** Face templates are encrypted. Only the owner and authorised managers can register or delete them.
Attendance records cannot be silently changed. Corrections are recorded with who made them and why.

**Your rights.** You can ask to see your data, correct it, or withdraw consent by contacting **[name, role, email/phone]**.

**Where the data is stored.** **[Hosting provider, region, e.g. Singapore]**.
