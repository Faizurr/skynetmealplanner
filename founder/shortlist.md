# Idea screen: B2C SaaS, 10 October 2026

The founder's criteria:
- Easy architecture.
- High demand, a "wow, I'd pay for this" product.
- Only 1 or 2 competitors, and weak ones.
- A target market of over 1 billion people.

## The honest finding first

In 2026, an idea that is easy to build and aimed at a billion people almost never
has only 1 or 2 competitors. If a vision LLM can do the job in one API call, there
are already a dozen small App Store clones of it. The general assistants (ChatGPT,
Gemini) are also a free substitute.

The gaps that do exist come in two kinds:
- jobs that take **action in the real world**, which is harder to clone than a
  chat answer;
- **countries and languages** the funded players ignore.

The screen below looks for that.

## The screen

Scored 1 to 5 against the founder's four criteria (higher is better). These are
research-based judgement calls, not measurements.

| idea | easy to build | demand / wow | few, weak competitors | 1B+ reach | notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A. AI that makes phone calls for you, any language** | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | Funded leader Pine AI is US/English, with thin, mixed reviews. Google's Call for Me is a US-only Pixel 11 preview. Apple's Hold Assist only waits on hold. Outside the US and English, almost nobody does it. |
| B. AI that explains any official letter | 5 | 3 | 2 | 4 | Many small clones (BriefAI, Explicame, ExplainThisDoc, X-plain, Mein Büro). A ChatGPT photo already does most of it. |
| C. Fair-price checker for quotes and invoices | 4 | 3 | 2 | 3 | Six or more recent clones (QuoteScore, Invoice Inspector, Ripoff.pro, Quoterly, QuotesBuster, FairBid). Good price data is the hard part. People use it rarely. |
| AI meal planner from a fridge photo (the founder's first idea) | 5 | 2 | 1 | 4 | Crowded: FridgeSmart, Frigo, PantryMynd, Chef AI, Samsung Food, Mealime and more. |
| AI screenshot organiser | 4 | 3 | 1 | 4 | Crowded: Screenary, CaptainShot, VizKeeper, ShotSort, RecallBox, Screenshot Swipe. Also platform risk (Pixel Screenshots). |
| Subscription cancel / bill negotiation | 3 | 4 | 2 | 2 | Rocket Money and Pine. Mostly a US problem. |
| Scam checker ("is this a scam?") | 5 | 3 | 1 | 5 | Free tools from security companies (Norton Genie, Bitdefender Scamio). Hard to charge. |

A, B and C went to the board (`founder/board/brief.md`) to be scored on all three
lenses.
