# Callwise (working name)

- **What it is:** An AI that makes your phone calls for you. You say what you
  need in your own language. It calls in the local language, gets it done and
  sends you the result with a transcript.
- **Who it is for:** First, expats and immigrants in one non-US country who must
  book appointments (doctors, dentists, government offices, services) in a
  language they don't speak. Later, anyone who dreads phone calls or hold queues.
- **What it sells, at what price:** Completed calls. Starting guess, untested:
  - 1 free call;
  - then $2.99 per completed call, or $12.99 a month for 10;
  - "no outcome, no charge".

  `/founder-pricing` and `/founder-cfo` set the real price once the prototype
  measures cost per call.
- **Where and how:** A mobile-friendly web app first; native apps later. Users
  arrive through expat communities, short-form video and search. Calls run on an
  off-the-shelf voice-agent platform plus Twilio telephony.
- **Budget and constraints:** Budget and team are not stated yet. Constraints set
  by the board:
  - one job (appointment booking) in version 1;
  - one country and language pair;
  - measure completion rate and cost on 50+ real calls before charging;
  - written legal view on AI disclosure and call recording before launch.
